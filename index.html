Frequently asked questions

Does PhotoBid replace my office staff?

PhotoBid handles repetitive intake and follow-up conversations so your team can focus on scheduling, customer service, and running the business.

Does PhotoBid give customers repair prices?

No. PhotoBid gathers information and helps coordinate service visits. Repair pricing and final diagnoses remain with your business.

Can PhotoBid understand pictures of garage doors?

Yes. PhotoBid can review customer-submitted photos to identify visible clues and guide the intake conversation. It does not replace a professional on-site inspection.

What happens when I miss a call?

PhotoBid can automatically send a text to begin collecting the customer's service information, subject to the business's configured phone and messaging setup.

Can PhotoBid schedule appointments?

PhotoBid helps collect scheduling information and move customers toward a technician visit. Direct booking capabilities depend on the available scheduling workflow and integrations.

Is PhotoBid built for other home service businesses?

PhotoBid is initially focused on garage door service companies.
