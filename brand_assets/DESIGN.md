# 1. Overview & Creative North Star

## "The Front Office That Never Misses"

PhotoBid is an AI-powered customer intake and scheduling assistant built specifically for garage door service companies.

The platform turns missed calls and inbound text messages into intelligent customer conversations. It uses specialized garage door knowledge, analyzes customer-submitted photos, asks relevant questions, and helps schedule technicians for service visits.

PhotoBid does **not** provide repair pricing or estimates to homeowners.

Its purpose is to help garage door businesses capture opportunities, qualify service requests, understand problems before dispatch, and reduce administrative work.

### Creative North Star

**"The Front Office That Never Misses."**

The website should feel like a modern, intelligent extension of a hardworking garage door business.

It should communicate:

- Reliability
- Speed
- Industry expertise
- Simplicity
- Professionalism
- Trust
- Practical business value

PhotoBid should not look like a generic AI startup or an enterprise CRM.

It should feel like software built by people who understand how garage door companies actually operate.

### Design Philosophy

Combine two complementary influences:

**Hatch — Messaging Inspiration**
- Direct, outcome-oriented headlines
- Simple explanations of complex automation
- Conversational product storytelling
- Clear demonstrations of workflows
- Strong emphasis on missed opportunities and customer engagement
- Short, confident copy

**Housecall Pro — Visual and Tonal Inspiration**
- Approachable, professional design
- Bright, welcoming visual language
- Strong typography
- High-contrast calls to action
- Service-business photography
- Friendly, confident copy
- Clear product illustrations
- Mobile-first presentation

The result should be modern, approachable, operationally credible, and conversion-focused.

# 3. Product Capabilities

## 3.1 Missed Call Text Back

When a customer calls a garage door business and nobody answers, PhotoBid can automatically initiate an SMS conversation.

### Customer Experience

Customer calls the business.

The call is missed.

PhotoBid sends a text:

"Hi! Sorry we missed your call. This is the service assistant for your garage door company. Are you having trouble with your garage door?"

The customer responds naturally.

PhotoBid begins collecting information.

### Business Value

- Recover missed opportunities
- Respond quickly
- Reduce manual callbacks
- Capture customer information
- Keep conversations moving outside business hours

### Marketing Language

**Every missed call deserves a second chance.**

PhotoBid automatically texts missed callers and starts the conversation while your team stays focused on the work in front of them.

---

## 3.2 Intelligent Garage Door Triage

PhotoBid is specialized for garage door service.

It understands common garage door systems and problems, including:

- Broken torsion springs
- Extension springs
- Garage door openers
- Rollers and tracks
- Cables and drums
- Door panels
- Sensors
- Remote controls
- Weather seals
- Off-track doors
- Doors that will not open or close
- Noisy or uneven operation

It uses this knowledge to ask better questions.

### Example

Customer:

"My garage door won't open."

PhotoBid:

"Got it. When you press the opener, do you hear the motor running, or does nothing happen?"

Customer:

"The motor runs but the door doesn't move."

PhotoBid:

"Thanks. If it's safe to do so, could you send a photo of the garage door and the opener? You don't need to touch or move anything."

The system gathers relevant information without pretending to replace a technician.

### Marketing Language

**Not just another chatbot. One that knows garage doors.**

PhotoBid understands the problems your customers describe and asks the right questions before your technician arrives.

### Safety Requirement

Never instruct homeowners to manipulate springs, cables, tracks, or other components under tension.

PhotoBid should not encourage dangerous DIY troubleshooting.

---

## 3.3 AI Photo Inspection

PhotoBid can analyze customer-submitted garage door photos.

Its purpose is to identify visible clues and improve service intake.

### Examples

- Potentially damaged panels
- Visible track misalignment
- Broken or loose components
- Opener-related issues
- General door condition
- Visible hardware concerns

Photo analysis should help determine what information the business needs before scheduling.

### Important Limitation

PhotoBid does not guarantee a diagnosis.

Photos may be incomplete, unclear, or insufficient.

The system should communicate uncertainty and escalate to a professional inspection when appropriate.

### Marketing Language

**A picture is worth a better service call.**

Customers send photos. PhotoBid reviews them, asks intelligent follow-up questions, and helps your team understand the issue before dispatch.

---

`150ms–250ms`

`300ms–500ms`
